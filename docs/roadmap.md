# Saipan launch, then other markets

Closed seed: Joeten, Payless, Superfresh, Ken's, convenience stores. 300–500 staples. Ugly receipts: glare, wrinkles, faded ink, long layouts.

Closed beta: 20–40 people who already shop. They photograph receipts. They do not type prices.

Public launch content: cheapest rice, milk comparisons, school-lunch basket, barbecue basket, weekly movers. Every share links to a product or basket page.

New market threshold (also in `app_settings`):

- 3 verified branches
- 2 trusted local contributors
- 25 accepted receipts
- 100 matched products
- under 10% unresolved submissions
- a local moderator
